# Ground Truth: Pollution

**What the filings say is in the air and water.**

An app in the Ground Truth series. Industrial facilities report their own toxic chemical releases to the EPA every year, into the Toxics Release Inventory (TRI) — data public by law under EPCRA §313. Pollution reads those self-reported filings and shows what the record says: no estimates, no modeling, just what was filed.

## The seed data

- **Greenville County, SC — reporting year 2024** (the most recent complete TRI year): 65 facilities filed active TRI reports, with combined on-site releases of **513,430 lb** — 371,517 lb to air, 141,780 lb to water, 132 lb to land (totals computed from the filing data).
- The app opens with the county's top 10 facilities by on-site releases, each with its chemicals and release routes, exactly as listed in TRI.
- Every figure comes from official EPA sources only (EPA Envirofacts / TRI). Unverifiable fields are shown as none — never guessed.

## How it works

Walk near a facility and see what it reported releasing — the radar layer is the series' signature. No ads, no accounts, no selling your data.
